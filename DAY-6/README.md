# Day 7 - Python Conditional Statements Practice

## About
Today, I practiced Python conditional statements by solving simple real-world and logical problems using `if`, `elif`, and `else`.

## Topics Covered
- Positive, negative, or zero
- Odd or even numbers
- Finding the largest of two numbers
- Finding the largest of three numbers
- Using logical operators (`and`)
- College admission eligibility
- Driving eligibility based on age and license
- Movie ticket pricing based on age
- Checking leap years

## Programs Included

### 1. Positive or Negative
Checks whether a number is positive, negative, or zero.

### 2. Odd or Even
Checks whether a number is odd or even using the modulo operator (`%`).

### 3. Largest of Two Numbers
Compares two numbers and displays the greater number.

### 4. Largest of Three Numbers
Uses conditional statements to find the largest number and handles some equal-number cases.

### 5. College Admission
Checks admission eligibility based on marks and entrance exam results.

### 6. Driving Eligibility
Checks whether a person meets the age requirement and has a valid driving license.

### 7. Movie Ticket Pricing
Calculates the ticket price based on age:
- Age 5 or below: Free
- Age 60 or above: ₹120
- Other ages: ₹200

### 8. Leap Year
Checks whether a year is a leap year using divisibility rules for 4, 100, and 400.

## What I Learned
I improved my understanding of conditional statements, comparison operators, logical operators, nested `if` statements, and decision-making in Python.
